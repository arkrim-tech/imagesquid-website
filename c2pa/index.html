C2PA photo manager

ImageSquid is a local-first photo manager with full C2PA support. This page is about what that means for a family archive, not for a newsroom desk.

What a Content Credential is

Provenance is how a collection verifies a picture's origins and that it is what it claims to be. C2PA Content Credentials are an open standard for signed, file-bound statements about image provenance.

What ImageSquid records

Every ImageSquid import starts a durable C2PA record per image. These records disclose human-generated content, AI-generated content, or a combination of both. Prior credentials, generator marks, and ImageSquid's later actions remain auditable by that industry-standard method.

Signed statements travel with the file. Free collection scans report missing, unsigned, and AI-touched items before you share.

ImageSquid also implements the W3C PROV-O PROVenance Ontology, a vocabulary for recording who did what to which resource. Those audit trails are human-readable and machine-readable. C2PA and PROV-O records underpin the parts of ImageSquid used to detect AI deepfakes and make image creation and processing more transparent.

Provenance stays with the archive. Each image's OCFL object can hold a metadata sidecar with C2PA, PROV-O, EXIF, Dublin Core, and Schema.org records.

If you need to share or publish an image with a third-party signed, certified C2PA watermark, ImageSquid.app can provide that service at a small fee.

A family archive

Photographs and videos are personal possessions, the kind of collection people spend years curating. Trust is harder when AI fakes are difficult to see. On a family archive, the import record is there so you can see whether a picture is human-generated, AI-generated, or both, and what was done to it later.
